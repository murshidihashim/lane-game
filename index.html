Lane Game · prototype

Test build. Simple shapes, no sound. You are blue (bottom); the bot is red (top).

Goal

Destroy the enemy Pusaka (the heirloom behind their towers). If time runs out, whoever destroyed more towers wins.

Your people and your lanes

  • Before the match you pick a species (Kampung, Hutan, Sungai, Serangga or Batu) and 2 heroes.
  • Every 20 s each lane sends a wave of one of your species' classes. Tap a wave chip (above your heroes) to change what marches in that lane.
  • Your cards are your species' classes, its spell, and Petir (lightning). Drag a card into a lane, or tap a card then a lane.
  • Cards cost Semangat (spirit, the purple beads). It refills by itself, twice as fast in the last minute.

Heroes

  • You pick 2 of 20 heroes before each match. Hero cards stay on the map all match, level up and come back after dying.
  • Each hero has a Trait (always on), a Power that fires by itself (the ring on its portrait), and an Ultimate at level 4, once per life (portrait shows ULT).
  • Drag a hero's portrait (above your cards) onto the map to give an order: Push or Gank (a lane), Defend (your tower), Farm (a ✦ camp), Retreat (your Pusaka). The ★ order is the hero's best: +15% damage.
  • A hero in the jungle is hidden from the enemy. Its first hit after that is an Ambush (double damage).
  • The middle lane is longer and winding. Its tower sits right in front of the Pusaka.

The map pays

  • Ward spots (stone cairns): a ward shows hidden enemy heroes for 90 s. Clearing an enemy ward pays.
  • Fruit groves on the river pay Semangat. Beast dens give a hero Jungle Fury (+20% damage).
  • Gajah Mina rises in the river at 1:30. Beat it and it joins you, charging down the middle lane.
  • Relics glow at the shrines every 45 s: Semangat, XP or a boost for the hero who grabs one.
  • Hero kills and towers pay Semangat; the first kill of the match pays extra.
