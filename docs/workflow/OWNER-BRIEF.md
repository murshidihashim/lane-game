## 1 · Try a change
1. You'll get a message that a change is ready and the checks are green.
2. Open the preview on your phone (refresh if it looks old) and follow the checklist.

## 2 · Checklist for PLAN-001 to PLAN-015 (about 30 minutes)
Write down what you **saw**, not just "it's fine".
- [ ] It opens in portrait. "How to play", then the species step, make sense.
- [ ] The draft: you and the bot take turns. Tap heroes to see them on the stage (Trait, Power,
      Ultimate tabs), lock in 3. Did it feel like a real draft? Then the versus screen.
- [ ] In the match, all three heroes walk out, one per lane. Tap a wave chip to change a lane's troops.
- [ ] Press a hero's panel (or hold the hero on the map): its orders fan out in a ring round your
      thumb. Slide onto one and let go, then tap a lit-up target. Try each: Move, Attack, Push, Gank,
      Defend, Farm, Ward, Retreat. Also try one swipe: slide out of the ring through Move and let go
      on a spot. Does the ring feel quick, and does the map stay clear?
- [ ] Watch a hero push alone: it should wait outside the enemy tower until your troops arrive, and
      go home to heal when badly hurt. Smart enough, or too independent?
- [ ] Open the Shop and buy items. At level 4, watch for "ULT NOW" and tap it.
- [ ] Is the fog clear? Can you read the characters and tower health on your phone?
- [ ] The map fills your screen's width and starts at your side. Drag it up and down to look around.
      Tap the magnifier (bottom-right) to see the whole map and again to come back; double-tap or
      pinch to look closer. Is this better than the smaller whole-map view? Does the pace feel right?
- [ ] The paper look: paper standees, batik board, indigo menus. Ultimates now look like cut paper
      too. Is everything easy to read on your phone?
- [ ] Watch a fight: arrows, stones and bolts flying; slashes; damage numbers; the dashed line to
      what each hero is hitting; pieces toppling when they die. Can you tell who hits whom?
- [ ] Watch your heroes talk: answers to orders, "Help!", "ready!", "spotted!". Too chatty, or useful?
- [ ] The new hero and troop designs: can you tell each hero apart at a glance by its big shape
  (hat, shield, umbrella, horns, wings)? Do they feel heroic enough, and are faces too small?
- [ ] Strategy and action (PLAN-015): drag a **ULT ⤴** button onto the map to aim it; dodge a red
  warning ring by flicking your hero; tap a glowing ✦ gem to fire a Power on cue; watch for COMBO
  stamps; HOLD a lane's waves and send them with GO; pick an upgrade when a level badge glows; try
  your two commander calls (beside the Shop). Too much to do with three heroes, or more fun?
      When a hero is off the screen, does its edge tab help? Does an ultimate's cast moment stand out?
- [ ] Play 5 or more matches with different species and heroes. Rematch keeps your line-up.
- [ ] Answer these questions:
  1. Do matches feel fair? Any species or hero still clearly too strong or too weak?
  2. Is the draft and versus screen exciting enough?
  3. Are 3 heroes manageable on your phone?
  4. Did you want to press Rematch?
- [ ] Anything confusing, slow or ugly: note the screen. Mention your phone model.

Send bugs with: device and browser, steps, expected, actual, how often, a screenshot, and the
"Match seed" from the result screen (it lets the match be replayed).

## 3 · What the checks mean
Green checks prove the automated tests passed. They do not prove the game is clear or fun, or
that it works on your real phone. Results are always PASS, FAIL, NOT RUN or NOT APPLICABLE.

## 4 · Publish (only when you decide)
