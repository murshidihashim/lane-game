Lane Game · prototype

Test build, no sound. You are blue (bottom); the bot is red (top).

You command, you don't deploy

  • Before the match you pick a species (Kampung, Hutan, Sungai, Serangga or Batu), then draft 3 heroes, taking turns with the bot: a hero can only be on one side. All three start on the map, one heading for each lane.
  • Every 20 s each lane sends a wave of one of your species' troop classes. Tap a wave chip (above your heroes) to change what marches in that lane. Waves grow stronger every minute.
  • There are no troop cards: you win with your lane choices and your heroes.

Heroes

  • Drag a hero's portrait onto the map to give an order: Push or Gank (a lane), Defend (your tower), Farm (a camp), Ward (a stone cairn), collect a relic, Retreat (your Pusaka), or drop it anywhere else to Move there and hold the spot. Or tap a portrait, then tap the map.
  • Each hero has a Trait (always on) and a Power that fires by itself. The ★ order is the hero's best: +15% damage.
  • Ultimate: unlocks at level 4, once per life. The ULT button glows when it is a good moment. Tap it and the hero fires at the next good moment (tap again to cancel).
  • Shop: spend Semangat (spirit, the purple count) on items for your heroes, 3 each. Semangat refills slowly; kills, towers, groves and relics pay more.

Seeing the map

  • The whole battlefield fits on the screen. To look closer, tap the magnifier in its corner, double-tap the map or pinch it; drag to look around while zoomed, and double-tap or tap the magnifier again to see everything.

Fog of war

  • You only see what your heroes, troops, towers and wards can see. The rest of the map is dark, and enemies there are hidden.
  • A hero in the jungle is hidden even closer. Its first hit after that is an Ambush (double damage).

The map pays

  • Wards on the stone cairns light up the fog for 90 s. Clearing an enemy ward pays.
  • Fruit groves on the river pay Semangat. Beast dens give a hero Jungle Fury (+20% damage).
  • Gajah Mina rises in the river at 1:30. Beat it and it joins you, charging down the middle lane.
  • Relics glow at the shrines every 45 s: Semangat, XP or a boost for the hero who grabs one.

Goal

Destroy the enemy Pusaka (behind their towers). Tower health shows on each tower and in the top bar. If time runs out, whoever destroyed more towers wins. If it's level, a minute and a half of sudden death follows: towers take triple damage and the next tower to fall wins.

Choose your people

Your species decides which troops march in your lanes. Then you draft 3 heroes against the bot.
