## FIVE WAYS TO MAKE THE MAP MORE ENGAGING (DEC-020, for the owner to choose)

Each idea adds a decision or a moment, not more buttons. The research found that earlier hybrid
games failed by adding load (REP-001).

1. **Monsoon, dry season and night.** The map changes about every 90 s, announced 10 s ahead:
   - monsoon floods the river, so only the bridges cross;
   - the dry season opens walkable shallows;
   - night shortens sight and makes ambushes stronger.

   You time pushes and ganks to the weather. *Effort: medium. Changes the rules (deterministic
   timer); needs tests and a balance run.*
2. **Kampung outposts.** Two or three jungle villages a hero captures by standing in them. They give
   vision, a little Semangat and a place to heal, and the enemy can retake them. This adds a
   constant tug of war for territory. *Effort: medium. Rules change; another thing to watch, so at
   most two or three.*
3. **Pasar malam merchant.** A night-market stall appears in the jungle now and then. It sells one
   rare item to the first hero who reaches it, which starts a race and a likely fight. *Effort:
   small to medium. Rules change; new items need balancing.*
4. **Lalang grass and startled birds.** Tall-grass patches beside the lanes hide whoever stands in
   them, for lane ambushes. Birds burst out of the grass and trees when a hidden hero passes, so
   you can read the fog. *Effort: medium. Rules change; must stay fair, and wards reveal the grass.*
5. **A living paper map.** Fireflies at night, rippling river paper and smoke from kampung kitchens.
   Towers collapse into paper scraps, and battle marks stay on the board. *Effort: small. Drawing
   only, no rules change.*

Suggested start: 5 (cheap, no rules change) together with 1 (the biggest change to how matches
feel). Because the map now scrolls, a small mini-map is also worth considering.
