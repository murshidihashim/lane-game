Lane Game · prototype

Test build. Simple shapes, no sound. You are blue (bottom); the bot is red (top).

Goal

Destroy the enemy Core. If time runs out, whoever destroyed more towers wins.

Cards

  • Drag a card into a lane (or tap a card, then tap a lane). It snaps onto the lane on your side.
  • Cards cost mana (purple bar). Mana refills by itself, twice as fast in the last minute.
  • Break an enemy tower to drop cards further up that lane.

Heroes

  • Ranger and Warden are hero cards. Once played they stay all match, level up and come back after dying.
  • Drag a hero's portrait (above your cards) onto the map to give an order: Push or Gank (a lane), Defend (your tower), Farm (a ✦ camp), Retreat (your Core).
  • A hero in the jungle is hidden from the enemy. Its first hit after that is an Ambush (double damage).
  • The middle lane is longer and winding. Its tower sits right in front of the Core.
