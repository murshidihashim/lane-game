## SUMMARY

- **World:** a rainforest archipelago inspired by Southeast Asian folk tales (DEC-006, proposed).
- **Roster:** twelve original heroes:
  - Rimba (ganker) and Benteng (defender), reworks of today's Ranger and Warden
  - Kail (pull), Seri (vision), Belang (jungle carry), Gergasi (siege)
  - Dalang (summoner), Senja (mage), Hujan (healer), Layang (flying diver), Pendekar (duelist),
    Pelanduk (trickster)
- **Power system (DEC-007, proposed):**
  - **Trait:** always on.
  - **Power:** automatic, with stronger ranks at levels 1–3.
  - **Ultimate:** unlocks at level 4, fires once per life, automatically.
  - **Best-order bonus:** +15% damage while the hero follows its preferred order.
  - **Pick 2:** you choose 2 heroes before each match.
  - **No dice:** no random critical hits or dodges.
- **Inspiration:**
  - From Dota: growth during a match, hooks, wards and smoke, duels, illusions and carries.
  - From Clash Royale: buildings-only win conditions, splash, spawners, flying troops,
    one-use abilities and readable cards.
- Every hero lists who it beats and who beats it.

## RISKS AND COSTS

- **Theme:** folklore themes need care. The concept sets rules to avoid religious figures, real
  people, spirits of the dead, and figures other games already use.
- **Scope:** Layang (flying) and Pendekar (duel) each need a new rule, so they come later.
- **Automatic ultimates** sometimes fire at a poor moment; the playtest will show how often.
