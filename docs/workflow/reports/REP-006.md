## SUMMARY

There are no troop cards any more. You play by commanding two heroes and choosing the troops for
each lane:
- **Hero commands:** move anywhere, gank, push, defend, farm, ward, collect a relic, retreat.
- **Items:** buy them for your heroes in the Shop.
- **Ultimates:** you call them, and the game suggests the moment.

Fog of war hides whatever your side can't see. Towers show their health clearly. Heroes and troops
are now drawn as characters, and every ultimate shows a banner and its own effect.
