## Decision details

### DEC-001 · Screen orientation

- **Question:** Portrait (one thumb, like Clash Royale) or landscape (like MLBB)?
- **Options:** Portrait · Landscape
- **Choice:** Portrait
- **Rationale:** One-thumb play like Clash Royale; all three lanes visible at once.
- **User impact:** Played upright with one hand.
- **Technical impact:** Field is tall and narrow; lanes are about a third of the screen width each.
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none
- **Approval:** Approved: “portrait” (2026-11-12)
- **Delivery:** Not started
- **Review trigger:** Players miss threats or hit the wrong lane in play tests.

### DEC-002 · Hero model

- **Question:** How do heroes work: a special card, a commander you move between lanes, or no hero?
- **Options:** Hero as a special card · Commander hero moved between lanes · No hero
- **Choice:** Hero cards that stay on the map; the player gives them orders (push, defend, gank, farm, retreat)
- **Rationale:** Adds Dota-style strategy (ganks, rotations, defence) without steering a hero by hand, which the research links to Star Wars: Force Arena failing.
- **User impact:** Drag a hero portrait onto the map to give an order; heroes fight on their own in between.
- **Technical impact:** Hero AI with five orders, levels, respawn, hidden while in the jungle.
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none
- **Approval:** Approved: “special carrds, with players having the option to instruct the heroes to gank/defend basically strategising like in dota” (2026-11-12)
- **Delivery:** Not started
- **Review trigger:** Play tests show orders feel like a chore or are ignored.

### DEC-003 · Middle lane shape

- **Question:** Should the three lanes be the same?
- **Options:** Three identical lanes · Longer winding middle lane, symmetrical map
- **Choice:** The middle lane is longer (it winds) but the map stays symmetrical
- **Rationale:** Gives the middle lane its own identity: slower to push, decisive when its tower falls, close to both jungles.
- **User impact:** Middle pushes take longer; breaking the middle tower exposes the Core.
- **Technical impact:** Lanes are waypoint paths; the map is the same when turned upside down.
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none
- **Approval:** Approved: “edit so that the mid lane is different, like make the middle lane longer but still simmetrical” (2026-11-12)
- **Delivery:** Not started
- **Review trigger:** The middle lane is always ignored or always the only lane that matters.

### DEC-007 · Hero power system v1

- **Question:** How do hero kits work?
- **Options:** Trait + Power + Ultimate at level 4 (once per life) + best-order bonus · One power only (as in the prototype) · Manual ultimate button
- **Choice:** Proposed: Trait (always on) + Power (automatic, ranks at level 1-3) + Ultimate (level 4, once per life, automatic) + best-order bonus (+15% damage); pick 2 heroes before a match; no random effects
- **Rationale:** Dota-style growth during a match with Clash Royale-style readability; keeps powers automatic as decided in DEC-004, so the player plays heroes through orders.
- **User impact:** Heroes get stronger in steps; level 4 is a power spike; a pick-2-heroes screen before matches.
- **Technical impact:** Ultimate flag per life, cooldown and ultimate rings on portraits, new effects (pull, stun, shield, reveal, transform).
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none until built
- **Approval:** Approved: “appriove power” (2026-11-12)
- **Delivery:** Implemented
- **Review trigger:** Playtests show powers firing at bad moments too often.

### DEC-008 · Creep species and lane waves

- **Options:** Species sets waves and cards (built) · Species sets lane waves only · Species sets troop cards only

- **Rationale:** Gives an RTS-style faction choice before the match and a lane-by-lane wave decision during it, while keeping Clash-style card play.
- **User impact:** A species pick before each match; a wave strip to choose each lane’s creep class.
- **Technical impact:** Species and class data, per-lane waves, unit abilities, species spells.
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none

### DEC-011 · No hand-played cards: play through heroes and lane troops

- **Question:** How does the player act during a match?
- **Options:** Heroes and lane troops only (built) · Keep troop cards as well · Keep only spells as cards
- **Choice:** No cards in hand. Both picked heroes start on the map. During the match the player: gives hero orders (move anywhere, push, gank, defend, farm, ward, collect relic, retreat); buys items for heroes with Semangat; calls each hero's ultimate (the game suggests the best moment, the hero fires it at that moment); and chooses the troop class for each lane's waves. Species keep their troop classes; species spells and Petir are removed. Replaces the hero-as-card part of DEC-002, the automatic ultimate of DEC-004/DEC-007 and the deck part of DEC-008.
- **Rationale:** The owner wants strategy to come only from commanding heroes and choosing lane troops, closer to a MOBA commander game.
- **User impact:** No card row. Two hero panels with health, items and an ULT button; a Shop; wave chips stay.
- **Technical impact:** Card, deck and spell systems removed; item stats, ult arming and a move order added; bot rewritten to match.
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none
- **Approval:** Approved: “remove troop cards being deployes by hand, players can only strategise by instructing heroes (move, buy items, ulti suggestion, gank) and selecting troops for each lanes ... introduce fog of war concept as well, clearly indicate health of the towers ... characters shouldnt be just dots, it needs to look like a character and ulti needs to visually show its effects on screen so players are aware on whats happening” (2026-11-12)
- **Delivery:** Implemented
- **Review trigger:** Playtests show too little to do, or matches stall without cards.

### DEC-012 · Fog of war

- **Question:** What can each player see?
- **Options:** Vision from your own pieces; enemies outside it are hidden (built) · Jungle hiding only (as before)
- **Choice:** Each side sees only around its own heroes, troops, towers, Pusaka and wards. Enemy heroes and troops outside that are not shown; the rest of the map is dimmed. Towers, the Pusaka and camps stay visible. The jungle still hides heroes inside vision unless a ward or a close enemy reveals them. The bot plays by the same vision for enemy heroes.
- **Rationale:** Makes wards, scouting and ganks matter; the owner asked for fog of war.
- **User impact:** Dark areas on the map; enemies appear when your pieces get close.
- **Technical impact:** A shared, whole-number vision check in the rules; a fog layer in drawing.
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none
- **Approval:** Approved: “remove troop cards being deployes by hand, players can only strategise by instructing heroes (move, buy items, ulti suggestion, gank) and selecting troops for each lanes ... introduce fog of war concept as well, clearly indicate health of the towers ... characters shouldnt be just dots, it needs to look like a character and ulti needs to visually show its effects on screen so players are aware on whats happening” (2026-11-12)
- **Delivery:** Implemented
- **Review trigger:** Players find the map too dark or lose track of the match.

### DEC-014 · Three heroes per player

- **Question:** How many heroes does each player command?
- **Options:** 3 heroes each (built) · 2 heroes each (as before)
- **Choice:** Each player picks 3 heroes before the match. All three start at the Pusaka, one heading for each lane (in 2-lane mode the third joins the left lane). Each has its own panel with health, items and an ULT button. No hero can be on both sides.
- **Rationale:** The owner asked for 3 heroes each; with no hand cards, a third hero gives the player more to command.
- **User impact:** Three hero panels; three picks in the draft.
- **Technical impact:** Hero count per player 3; HUD for 3 panels; bot and economy handle 3 heroes.
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none
- **Approval:** Approved: “do all the balance tunes first, each character should select 3 heroes, the loading screen where you select heroes should be like dota/tekken” (2026-11-12)
- **Delivery:** Implemented
- **Review trigger:** Playtests show 3 heroes are too many to manage on a phone.

### DEC-015 · Draft-style hero select and versus screen

- **Question:** How does the hero selection screen look and work?
- **Options:** Draft screen with turns, big hero preview, roster grid and a versus screen (built) · Simple picker (as before)
- **Choice:** After choosing a species, a draft: you and the bot take turns picking (you 1, bot 1, you 2, bot 1... a snake order), shown as two rows of 3 slots facing each other. The highlighted hero stands large on a lit stage with its name, role and powers; a roster grid of 20 portraits below; picked heroes are taken. Then a versus screen shows both line-ups and species, with a short loading bar, before the match. Original Hikayat look (night, songket gold), no borrowed art.
- **Rationale:** The owner wants the selection to feel like a fighting-game or MOBA draft: a moment of build-up and counter-picking.
- **User impact:** A dramatic pick screen and a versus screen before each match.
- **Technical impact:** New draft screen module; deterministic bot draft picks so matches still replay.
- **Data and privacy:** None.
- **Cost:** none
- **Approval:** Approved: “do all the balance tunes first, each character should select 3 heroes, the loading screen where you select heroes should be like dota/tekken” (2026-11-12)
- **Delivery:** Implemented
- **Review trigger:** The draft feels slow on repeat matches (a quick rematch skips it).
