## SUMMARY

The balance pass came first. Balance was tuned on the 3-hero game, so the tuning holds for what
you play.
- Every species now wins 47–58% of bot matches.
- Every hero now wins 42–62% (before: Dalang 85%, Batu 71%, Kampung 29%).
- Draws are 8%, and matches no longer end very early.

Each player now drafts 3 heroes, taking turns with the bot, on a fighting-game-style select screen.
A versus screen plays before the match.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **Balance tool:** `npm run balance` plays 1,200 bot matches and fails if the targets are missed.
- **Bug fix:** towers now shoot back at long-range attackers. Before, Batu's sling and Helang could
  hit a tower from just outside its reach.
- **Bot:**
  - It sends building-breaker troops only into lanes it is winning.
  - In sudden death, all its heroes go for the weakest enemy tower.
- **Pace:**
  - Waves grow 15% a minute.
  - Towers have 1,900 health.
  - Towers take triple damage in sudden death.
- **Species:**
  - Kampung, Sungai and Hutan made stronger.
  - Batu weaker: 2 pebbles per wave, a softer golem and sling.
  - Serangga's queen hatches more slowly; its ants and hornets are sturdier.
- **Heroes:**
  - Weaker: Dalang, Rimba, Helang.
  - Stronger: Saudagar, Kail, Pelanduk, Senja, Gergasi, Belang, Buaya, Tedung.
- **Three heroes (DEC-014):**
  - They start one per lane.
  - Three hero panels.
  - No hero can be on both sides.
- **Draft (DEC-015):**
  - Species step, then turns (you 1, bot 2, you 2, bot 1).
  - The highlighted hero stands large on a lamp-lit stage, with its powers in tabs.
  - A 20-portrait roster.
  - A versus screen with both line-ups.
