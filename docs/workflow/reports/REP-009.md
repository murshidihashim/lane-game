## SUMMARY

The "heroes stop after an order" problem was a real bug with two causes, and both are fixed:
- Heroes were being shoved backwards by their own troops marching the other way.
- Two heroes meeting head-on on the same line pushed straight against each other and froze.

Heroes now also think a little for themselves. Commanding is now: tap the hero, pick an order, tap
a lit-up target.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **Bug fix:**
  - Heroes walk through their own side.
  - Pieces that bump head-on slide past each other.
  - Found with a stall detector: 60 matches of random orders went from 14 stalls to 0. The detector
    is now a permanent soak test.
- **Smarter heroes (DEC-017):**
  - They wait just outside an enemy tower until their own troops are in its reach.
  - They head home to heal below 25% health while pushing, and go back at 80%.
  - They go for enemy heroes in reach first, then the weakest enemy.
  - The hero panel shows when a hero is waiting or falling back.
- **Attack order:** send a hero after an enemy you can see. It returns to its job afterwards, and
  stops if the enemy vanishes into the fog.
- **Command menu:**
  - Tap a hero's panel, or the hero on the map, and 8 orders appear: Move, Attack, Push, Gank,
    Defend, Farm, Ward, Retreat. The ★ marks the hero's best order.
  - Picking one lights up only the valid targets: lanes, red rings on enemies, markers on towers,
    camps and cairns. A hint and Cancel are shown.
  - Retreat is instant.
  - Dragging a portrait still works as a shortcut.
