## SUMMARY

Demon Slayer's look has three parts:
- painterly anime characters;
- effects drawn like traditional art (its makers cite ukiyo-e woodblock prints);
- one traditional pattern per hero.

Option 6 uses the same approach with Malay art: anime heroes, a signature Malay pattern each, and
ultimates drawn as batik, songket or wayang art. This suits the owner's taste and stays original.
