## SUMMARY

The five styles:
1. Hikayat board game (today's look, painted by an artist).
2. Toy-box 3D.
3. Wayang kulit "living puppets".
4. Batik paper-cut diorama.
5. Anime cel-shaded 2D.

Recommendation:
- Keep Hikayat while the game changes.
- For the full game, use wayang living puppets on a batik-coloured board (3 with parts of 4).
- Runner-up: toy-box 3D, if the aim is the widest reach and there is a 3D art budget.

Full write-up and sources: `docs/research/art-style-options.md`.
