## SUMMARY

**What is missing today:**
- Fights, Powers and ultimates resolve by themselves. Your hands rarely decide a fight.
- The ULT button fires "at the next good moment", so even the biggest moment is timed by the game.
- Strategy exists (lane troops, orders, items, map rewards), but there are few choices with a clear
  trade-off or a surprise for the opponent.

**The limit we keep:** the research in REP-001 found that real-time card and MOBA hybrids failed
by adding mental load. The genre leader also removed cooldown abilities from its heroes in August
2026. So every idea below keeps an automatic default. Each one adds a few big moments rather than
constant tapping, and with three heroes that matters.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

### Action: your hands decide the big moments

1. **Aim your ultimate.**
   - Hold ULT, drag onto a target or spot, and let go. A plain tap still fires it at a good moment.
   - About half the ultimates gain an aim:
     - a target: Coil, Duel, Hunter's Mark, Grand Performance;
     - a spot: Dawn Freeze, Riptide's direction, Moon Kite's landing, Unbreakable's tower.
   - Self and team ultimates (Veil, Golden Monsoon, Swamp King...) stay one tap.
   - *Why:* the one big moment per life becomes a skill shot. *Effort:* medium.
2. **Warning rings and a dodge dash.**
   - Big enemy attacks show a red paper ring about 1 s before they land: aimed ultimates and Gajah
     Mina's charge.
   - Flick a hero to dash a short way, once every 10 s.
   - The bot dodges only what its side can see.
   - *Why:* reaction and outplay; you can see a fight coming. *Effort:* medium. Best with idea 1.
3. **Power on your cue.**
   - Each hero panel shows its Power gem.
   - Tap it to fire now for +20%. Left alone, the Power still fires by itself as today.
   - *Why:* timing a stun or a hook pays. *Effort:* small.
   - *Risk:* three gems to watch. This is exactly the load the research warns about, so try it last.
4. **Combo chains.**
   - Powers and ultimates from different heroes that hit the same enemies within 2 s chain into a
     combo: +15% a step, with a paper "COMBO ×2" stamp.
   - *Why:* rewards grouping heroes and ordering stun then damage. *Effort:* small to medium.

### Strategy: bigger choices with trade-offs

5. **Rally the waves.**
   - Hold a lane's waves at your fort (up to 3), then release them as one big push with a war drum.
   - The build-up is hidden by the fog unless the enemy has a ward there.
   - *Why:* feints, timing a push with your heroes' ultimates, reading the enemy. *Effort:* medium.
   - It uses the wave chips you already have, so no new screen.
6. **Level-up choices.**
   - At levels 2 and 3 each hero offers two upgrades for its Power, picked on its panel. For
     example, Kail's Hook gets longer reach or a longer stun.
   - The game picks for you after 8 s.
   - *Why:* builds change from match to match to counter the enemy draft. *Effort:* medium (40
     upgrades to write and balance).
7. **Commander calls.**
   - Before the match, pick 2 of about 6 commander abilities: War Drum (troops faster), Bamboo
     Barricade (blocks a lane briefly), Spirit Eye (reveals a spot), and others.
   - They cost Semangat, so they compete with items, and they have long cooldowns.
   - *Why:* a commander's own decisive move. *Effort:* medium to large.
   - *Note:* these are not cards (no hand, no deck), but they add a new kind of action, so they
     need their own decision against DEC-011.
8. **Buyback and bounties.**
   - Pay Semangat to bring a fallen hero back at once.
   - A hero on a kill streak carries a bounty that pays whoever ends it.
   - *Why:* comebacks and "hunt the dangerous hero" decisions. *Effort:* small.
