## SUMMARY

You pick 2 of 20 heroes before each match; the bot picks 2 at random. Each hero has the power
system approved in DEC-007:
- a **Trait** that is always on
- a **Power** that fires automatically, with ranks at levels 1–3
- an **Ultimate** at level 4 that fires once per life
- a **best order** worth +15% damage

Names follow DEC-006:
- the twenty heroes
- Semangat (mana) and Pusaka (Core)
- the troop cards: Silat Guards, Pemanah, Kerbau, Kelip-kelip, Perisai and Petir

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **Roster:** the 12 heroes from concept v1, plus 8 new ones: Saudagar (economy), Badak (charger),
  Tedung (poison), Tukang (builder), Helang (marksman), Embun (fog and freeze), Ombak (lane wave)
  and Buaya (life-steal).
- **Rules (`src/game/`):**
  - New effects: stun, silence, pull, push-back, knock-back, shields, heals, damage over time,
    marks, haste, attack speed, immunity, take-over of enemy troops, duels, decoys, summons,
    flying (only ranged units, towers and spells hit fliers), and lane or area zones (fog, flood,
    tide, lanterns).
  - Each hero's kit is in `heroes.ts`, and its card text is in `rules.ts`.
  - Everything is still whole-number maths, so the same seed and the same moves replay exactly.
- **Bot:** gives orders that suit each hero's best order.
- **Screen:**
  - A hero pick screen.
  - Portraits show a power cooldown ring and a ULT label when the ultimate is ready.
  - The ★ marks the best order.
  - The map shows status marks, zones, summons, fliers' shadows and duel rings.
  - Pop-up texts stack instead of overlapping.
- **Docs:** `docs/design/heroes.md` is generated from the game data, and `npm run verify` fails if
  it drifts. Concept v1 is marked superseded.
