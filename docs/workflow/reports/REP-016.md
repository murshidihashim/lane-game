## SUMMARY

Fights now show who hits whom and how:
- shots fly by type, melee hits slash, and hero fights show damage numbers;
- a line marks what each hero is hitting;
- ultimates have a cast moment on the hero.

Your heroes also talk back in speech bubbles, and off-screen heroes appear as tabs at the map's edge.
The game rules are unchanged; the bot-vs-bot results are identical to before.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **How they attack:** each ranged attacker throws its own paper projectile:
  - arrows, lobbed sling stones, water jets, spit, coins, bamboo darts, star-bolts and tower darts;
  - melee hits sweep a paper crescent, and heavy hitters thud;
  - paper confetti bursts on every hit;
  - attackers face their target and lunge on the strike.
- **Where they attack:**
  - a dashed line and reticle from each fighting hero (gold for yours, red when an enemy hero hits
    your side);
  - damage numbers on hero fights;
  - the hero panel flashes red when hit and shows "⚔ vs Belang".
- **Ultimates:** a charge ring while called; on firing, paper rays, a pop and flash, and the
  ultimate's name shouted; a bobbing ULT badge when ready.
- **Heroes talk back:**
  - bubbles answer every order;
  - they call out waiting for troops, falling back, Help!, a ready ultimate, enemies spotted and
    coming back into the fight;
  - bubbles stack instead of overlapping.
- **Readable status:** stun stars, a silenced bubble, poison drops and a frost ring.
- **Off-screen heroes:** an edge tab with the portrait (red when hurt, gold when casting); tap it to
  look there.
- **Deaths:** standees topple backwards in paper.
- **Game events:** hits now carry the attacker, target and damage, and deaths say what died. This is
  drawing data only.
