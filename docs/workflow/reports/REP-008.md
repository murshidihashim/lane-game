## SUMMARY

The owner wasn't sure whether the map felt small or the pieces too fast, so both were addressed:
- Everyone now walks about 30% slower.
- The map can be zoomed up to 2×.

The game was re-balanced for the slower pace. The characters were redrawn and animated more richly.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **Pace (DEC-016):**
  - Every troop and hero walks at 70% of its old speed; attacks and powers are unchanged.
  - Walking steps follow the distance actually walked, so slow pieces plod and fast ones scamper.
- **Zoom (DEC-016):**
  - The whole map shows by default.
  - The magnifier (bottom-right), a double-tap or a pinch zooms to 2×, and a drag pans while zoomed.
  - The map is painted sharper so zooming stays crisp, and characters grow with the zoom.
- **Balance:** slower walking meant fewer towers fell, and draws rose to 19%. Fixes:
  - towers at 1,700 health;
  - waves grow 18% a minute;
  - sudden death lasts 1:30 (60 s was too short for slower pieces);
  - Batu and Sungai trimmed; Hutan, Serangga and Kampung nudged up.
- **Characters:**
  - Cel shading, faces with expressions, songket trims.
  - A team pennant on every hero.
  - 8-frame walks: real four-leg gaits, wing beats, slithering, rolling.
  - Idle breathing; wind-up, strike swoosh and follow-through.
  - A white flash when hit, and a dust-puff arrival.
