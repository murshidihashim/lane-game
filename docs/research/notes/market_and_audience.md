## 1. Global mobile games market 2024–2026, sub-genre trends, and 2025–2026 hits and flops in real-time PvP strategy

### Takeaway
Mobile is just over half of a roughly $200B games market, and Newzoo's final 2025 figure is $113.3B, up 10.7%. Sensor Tower's store-IAP view, however, is flat: $81.75B in 2025, up 1.3%. Strategy is the largest IAP genre. In real-time PvP, 2025 was a year of incumbent revival (Clash Royale) and big-studio new-IP failures (Squad Busters, Warcraft Rumble). New entrants struggle even with huge budgets.

### Cited Findings
**Market size (Newzoo, final 2025 figures, published around early 2026)**
- Global games revenue was $201.6B in 2025 (+9.1% YoY). Mobile was $113.3B (+10.7% YoY). Newzoo forecasts mobile at $121.1B in 2026 and the whole market at $234.4B by 2028 — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/global-games-revenue-surpasses-200bn-for-the-first-time-mobile-generates-113bn/); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/global-games-revenue-breached-200b-in-2025-newzoo/); [LevelUp](https://www.levelup.com/en/news/video-game-industry-hits-record-2016-billion-in-2025-yet-console-stagnation-and-layoffs-persist)
- **Conflicting earlier Newzoo editions for the same year:**
  - The September 2025 forecast put 2025 mobile at $103.1B (+2.9%). A December 2025 revision put it at $108B (+7.7%).
  - Earlier total-market forecasts were $188.8B–$189B, then $197B.
  - Always cite the edition being used — [PocketGamer.biz (revision to $188.9bn)](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/newzoo-revises-2025-global-games-market-growth-forecast-to-1889bn); [PocketGamer.biz ($197bn)](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/the-global-games-market-is-forecast-to-reach-197bn-in-2025); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/newzoo-raises-global-games-market-2025-forecast-to-197-billion)

**Market size (Sensor Tower, app-store IAP only)**
- 2024: global mobile game IAP revenue was $82B (+4% YoY). Strategy and RPG were the top-grossing genres globally. Casual leads in the US/West — [Sensor Tower press release](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-mobile-gaming-rebounds-in-2024-as-player-engagement-and-spending-reach-new-highs)

- **Hit: the Clash Royale revival.** See section 2 for details. Supercell called Clash Royale its standout success of 2025, with new-player growth of nearly 500% — [Supercell CEO letter](https://supercell.com/en/news/the-best-games-havent-been-made-yet/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/supercell-revenue-declines-4-to-265bn-in-2025/)

  - It said the game "did not reach the long-term quality bar". Purchases ended, and servers close in 2026 "not before the middle of the year".
  - Wikipedia lists the server shutdown as Dec 14, 2026.
  - Sources: [Supercell news](https://supercell.com/en/news/squad-development-ending/); [Supercell support](https://support.supercell.com/squad-busters/en/articles/squad-busters-is-ending.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squad_Busters)
- Squad Busters' 2025 revenue was estimated at only $8.5M. Supercell's new 2025 game mo.co made about $4.7M. Both are third-party estimates — [GamingonPhone on X](https://x.com/GamingonPhone/status/1998265294217953620). A blog claims Squad Busters went "from $23M to 0 in 18 months"; this is unverified — [Medium](https://medium.com/@GamerlandInterviewer/from-23m-to-0-how-squad-busters-collapsed-in-18-months-892432dfc21a)
- **Flop: Warcraft Rumble (Blizzard's Clash-like lane/minis game, 2023).** On July 2, 2025, Blizzard stopped developing new content and laid off most of the team — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warcraft_Rumble)
- **Supercell overall:**
  - 2025 non-GAAP revenue fell 4% to €2.65B (about $3.0B). EBITDA rose 6% to €932M.
  - The portfolio has about 290M monthly active players.

  - Brawl Stars store sales reportedly fell by up to 57% in 2025 (third-party store data).
  - Sources: [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/supercell-revenue-declines-4-to-265bn-in-2025/); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/supercell-revenue-falls-to-301-billion-in-2025); [MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/supercells-2025-revenue-dipped-but-profits-rose-and-clash-royale-had-a-big-year)
- **Honor of Kings** was among 2025's top-grossing mobile games worldwide — [MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/). Its 2025 revenue is estimated at $1.6B–$1.68B, against $1.74B in 2024. These AppMagic-based estimates come via an aggregator, and the scope (with or without China) is unclear — [Icon Era](https://icon-era.com/statistics/honor-of-kings/)

### Inferences
- The mobile "growth" headline depends on the source. Newzoo's +10.7% includes more than store IAP. Sensor Tower's store IAP grew only about 1%. A small team should plan for a mature market in which share is taken from incumbents, not from new spend.
- Strategy is the largest IAP genre, which is good for a strategy-PvP hybrid. But strategy revenue is dominated by 4X and SLG titles, not real-time card PvP. The relevant sub-genre (real-time card/lane PvP) is effectively a handful of incumbents: Clash Royale, MLBB, Honor of Kings and Brawl Stars.
- Two well-funded studios (Supercell and Blizzard) failed with new real-time PvP/lane IPs in 2024–2025. Meanwhile, the 9-year-old Clash Royale grew about 2.5x. Players in this genre are loyal to established games and hard to win away with new IP. The hybrid needs a clearly differentiated hook and very low UA cost, not head-on competition.

### Gaps
- No verified market-share figures were found for "card battler" or "MOBA" as separate sub-genres in 2025. Secondary summaries cite Strategy at 21.4% of IAP from 4% of downloads, and Strategy $17.5B and RPG $16.8B in 2025. These could not be traced to an identifiable primary page, so they are excluded.
- Global 2026 full-year actuals are not yet available, since the date is Nov 2026.
- Searches surfaced no 2025–2026 small-team real-time PvP strategy hit.

## 2. Clash Royale's trajectory 2023–2026; MLBB's scale and SEA dominance; Honor of Kings' 2024 global launch

### Takeaway
Clash Royale went from about $12M a month in February 2025 to a $67.8M peak in September 2025, a 5.7x rise (AppMagic). Full-year 2025 estimates range from $422M to $647M depending on the tracker. The comeback was driven by economy and balance fixes plus new modes, mostly through organic growth. MLBB remains SEA's dominant MOBA: more than 110M MAU (company claim), about $190M a year in tracked store IAP, and record esports viewership (M7: 5.68M peak, January 2026). Honor of Kings' 2024 global launch gained huge SEA installs but did not take revenue leadership from MLBB.

  - February 2025 was a low of $12M. Growth accelerated in May and peaked at $67.8M in September 2025, a 5.7x rise. The daily peak was $6.6M.

  - Source: [AppMagic blog](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/ClashRoyale-Comeback)

### Inferences
- If secondary sites' "+149% to $646.8M" is AppMagic's figure, it implies Clash Royale's 2024 revenue was about $260M (646.8 / 2.49), a low point for the title. The $422M vs $647M split for 2025 is probably net vs gross of store fees, but this is unconfirmed.
- Lesson from Clash Royale: the revival came from economy and design fixes (less reward inflation, better balance, fresh modes, 2v2) and organic and creator reach, not from paid UA. A small team can copy this cheaply: a tight economy plus social modes plus creator-friendly moments.
- **Malaysia is a high-value SEA market.** For HoK, Malaysia was 5.4% of downloads but 14.4% of ex-China IAP. That is roughly 2.7x its download share, compared with about 0.3x for Indonesia and about 0.24x for the Philippines. Indonesia and the Philippines are volume and virality markets; Malaysia is a monetization market.
- MLBB's tracked store IAP of about $190M against 110M+ claimed MAU works out to under $2 per MAU per year. The real figure is higher once D2C top-ups are counted. The MOBA audience in SEA is huge but low-ARPU, and monetizes mainly through cosmetics and passes.
- HoK's launch apparently did not cannibalise MLBB. Buffget's +48% MAU in November 2025 and MLBB's record M7 viewership in January 2026 support this. Even Tencent could not dislodge the incumbent, which reinforces positioning the hybrid as complementary (short sessions, card-strategy hook) rather than as "another MOBA".
- MLBB's esports peaks (4–5.7M) are over 15x Clash Royale-scale events (not measured here). An esports-flavoured identity (MPL culture and team play) resonates with the SEA audience.

### Gaps
- Searches surfaced no verified Clash Royale annual revenue for 2023 or 2024, and no reliable 2025 Clash Royale MAU figure. A July 2025 "54.7M" figure from an aggregator table is garbled.
- No verified MLBB 2025 download count was found, nor a verified Moonton primary source for the 110M MAU figure.
- No 2025 head-to-head revenue for HoK vs MLBB in Indonesia, the Philippines or Malaysia was found, and no MPL Malaysia 2025 viewership numbers.
- The date of the Savvy–Moonton deal is unconfirmed.

## 3. Southeast Asia mobile gaming: market size, players, devices, data costs, ARPU, payments, and Malaysia specifically

### Takeaway
SEA-6 games revenue was about $5.6B in 2025 (Niko), across about 290M players. That is only about $19 per player per year, a high-volume, low-ARPU market. Store IAP is about $625M a quarter (Sensor Tower, Q1 2025), with Thailand, Indonesia and Malaysia the top three. Malaysia is unusual: smaller in players (about 19–25M gamers) but #3 in SEA store IAP, with a high iOS share (roughly 37–46% of mobile web traffic). Payments are e-wallets, carrier billing and D2C top-up shops, not cards.

### Cited Findings
**Market size and players**
- Niko Partners on SEA-6 (Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam):
  - Games revenue was more than $5.6B in 2025, up 4.7% YoY.
  - Revenue is forecast to reach about $7.0B by 2030, a 4.8% CAGR.
  - Source: [Niko Partners substack](https://substack.nikopartners.com/p/7-billion-in-2030-opportunities-in)
- Players numbered 285.82M in 2024 (+3.1%) and 290M in 2025 (+1.5%), with 324.4M forecast by 2029. An earlier Niko forecast had $5.5B in 2025 rising to $6.4B in 2029 — [GameDevReports on Niko](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/niko-partners-southeast-asia-games)
- Niko says Malaysia, Thailand and Vietnam are the fastest-growing SEA markets, and it lowered its forecasts for Thailand and Indonesia — [GameDevReports on Niko](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/niko-partners-southeast-asia-games)
- **Conflict:** Newzoo puts SEA at $6.2B in consumer spending for 2024, and one summary cites "300M+" SEA players — [GameDevReports on Niko](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/niko-partners-southeast-asia-games)
- Niko puts Asia + MENA at $88.97B in 2025 and $103.6B by 2030, with a player base nearing 2B — [Branding in Asia](https://www.brandinginasia.com/asia-and-mena-games-revenue-predicted-to-surpass-100-billion-by-2030/); [MobileMarketingReads](https://www.mobilemarketingreads.com/asia-and-mena-gaming-revenue-projected-to-reach-103-6b-by-2030-as-player-base-nears-2-billion/)

  - SEA was the #2 region globally for game downloads, at 1.93B.
  - Indonesia had 870M (+9% QoQ), about 45% of the SEA total.
  - SEA game IAP revenue was $625M in Q1 2025: Thailand $162M, Indonesia $118M, Malaysia $103M. D2C payments are excluded.
  - MOBA led Indonesia's QoQ revenue growth (+32.7%), and MOBA was also the top QoQ grower in the Philippines (+19%).
  - Sources: [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/southeast-asia-mobile-gaming-2025); [Tiger Brokers news](https://www.itiger.com/news/1163935920)

- **Platform split:** Malaysia is far more iOS-heavy than the rest of SEA. In Kuala Lumpur, an iOS build cannot be skipped; in Indonesia and the Philippines, Android-first is fine.

- **Payment readiness:** plan for D2C web top-up alongside store billing (Codashop-style resellers, e-wallets and carrier billing). Store-IAP trackers undercount SEA spend, and a D2C web shop also improves margins for a small team.

## 4. Player preferences in SEA: sessions, competitive PvP and esports, social/squad play, genres, pay-to-win vs cosmetics, gacha

### Takeaway
SEA's top genres are MOBA, MMORPG and strategy. Competitive team play is culturally central: MLBB pulls 2–5.7M peak esports viewers, and 7 in 10 Malaysian parents now support gaming careers. Evidence on pay-to-win attitudes is mostly indirect. Pan-Asian research shows cosmetics are far more accepted than paid power in competitive modes, and MOBAs are expected to be "play-to-win". No hard SEA session-length data was found.

### Cited Findings
- SEA's most popular genres are MOBA, MMORPG and strategy. MLBB's cosmetics-led model is described as especially profitable in the region; this is a marketing blog's inference, not survey data — [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/monetizing-mobile-games-in-asia)
- **Niko Partners and Google report:**
  - 1 in 4 mobile gamers surveyed in China, Japan, South Korea and India had paid for gear to gain a competitive advantage.
  - Cosmetic or shareable purchases are "much more accepted by gamers in Asia because it doesn't falsely give an advantage".
  - Competition in MOBAs and battle royales is "geared toward play-to-win experiences that remain the same for everyone regardless of spend".
  - SEA was not in the sample.
  - Source: [Think with Google / Niko Partners PDF](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/10187/Google-Niko-Partners_The-Art-of-Monetizing-Games-in-Asia.pdf); [Niko Partners](https://nikopartners.com/from-playing-to-paying-the-art-of-monetizing-games-in-asia/)
- A small 2025 Indonesian study of 81 gacha players found the most prevalent features were cosmetic rewards, in-game currency, pay-to-win mechanics, cash-out options and rarity — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379065323_Antecedents_of_Gacha_gaming_intention_Extending_UTAUT2_with_structural_video_game_characteristics)
- **Playtime:** SEA-6 players averaged about 15 hours a week in 2025, down from 21.8 hours in 2024 (Niko, via search summary) — [GameDevReports on Niko](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/niko-partners-southeast-asia-games)
- **Esports culture:** see section 2 (M7 5.68M peak; MPL Indonesia 4.13M peak and 113M hours watched; MSC as the top title at EWC 2025 and 2026). Esports is also a medal event at the 2025 SEA Games in Bangkok — [Niko Partners](https://substack.nikopartners.com/p/bangkok-brings-the-heat-of-esports)
- **Malaysia social attitudes:** the Niko/Moonton bulletin (November 2025, supported by Malaysia's Ministry of Youth and Sports and Esports Integrated) reports that 7 in 10 Malaysian parents support gaming as a legitimate career path. The bulletin is sponsor-linked — [Niko Partners](https://nikopartners.com/bulletins-white-papers/societal-impact-of-mobile-legends-bang-bang-in-malaysia/); [The Gaming Districts](https://thegamingdistricts.com/selangor-red-giants-og-esports-make-history-with-fourth-straight-mpl-malaysia-title-as-new-mlbb-research-reveals-massive-shift-in-parental-support/)
- **Social and squad modes drive engagement:** Clash Royale's 2025 surge coincided with new multi-player modes (4-player Merge Tactics) and the return of Classic 2v2 — [Supercell](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashroyale/blog/release-notes/june-update-2025/); [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/clash-royale-statistics/)
- The Niko Livestreaming Tracker for February 2026 was headlined "The Reign of RPGs", suggesting RPGs led SEA/Asia livestream viewing that month. Only the headline was available — [Niko Partners](https://substack.nikopartners.com/p/the-reign-of-rpgs-niko-partners-livestreaming)

### Inferences
- **Positioning:** a "MOBA-strategy you can play in 3–5 minutes" fits SEA's MOBA literacy and the drop in weekly playtime (21.8 to 15 hours, if accurate). A shorter-session competitive game may fill gaps between MLBB sessions instead of replacing them.
- **Monetization stance:** for a competitive PvP game in SEA, lead with cosmetics (skins, emotes, arenas, card backs) and a battle pass. Keep card-level power gaps small or capped in ranked play. MLBB's cosmetics-led success and the Asia-wide acceptance of cosmetics support this. Clash Royale's 2025 fix (less reward inflation, better balance) suggests progression economies need tight tuning.
- **Social:** squad, duo or 2v2 modes and clan or guild features match both MLBB's 5v5 team culture and the Clash Royale evidence. 2v2 lanes with a friend are a natural format for a lane-based card game.
- **Esports aspiration:** SEA players and even parents treat MLBB esports as mainstream. Spectator-friendly match design (readable lanes, short matches, comeback moments) enables community tournaments and creator content, a cheap growth lever.

### Gaps
- Searches surfaced no hard data on typical SEA mobile session lengths or match-length preferences.
- No direct SEA survey on pay-to-win vs cosmetics attitudes or gacha acceptance was found (the Niko/Google sample excludes SEA). The NTU survey covers SEA but focuses on subscriptions and wallets.
- An unsourced claim that free-to-play was 75.62% of SEA's gaming market in 2025 was excluded.

## 5. Monetization, retention and UA cost benchmarks (SEA vs West)

### Takeaway
The median mobile game keeps about 22% of players on Day 1, under 4% by Day 7 and under 1% by Day 30. Top-decile games reach about 40% D1. SEA installs cost a fraction of US ones: roughly $0.15–$0.90 vs $1.50–$7.68 depending on genre and OS. That makes SEA a cheap place to test retention, but low ARPU means LTV there rarely funds large paid UA.

### Cited Findings
**Retention (GameAnalytics)**
- Median D1 is about 22%, D7 just under 4% (3.42–3.94% across summaries), and D30 about 0.7–0.8%.
- The top 10% of games reach about 40% D1. The top 1% reach 64–68% D1 and 13–15% D30.
- Top-quartile D1 is 31–33% on iOS vs 25–27% on Android.
- The 2025 edition measured D28 and used classic rather than rolling retention.
- Sources: [GameAnalytics 2026 benchmarks](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-mobile-pc-gaming-benchmarks); [GameDevReports on GameAnalytics 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-gaming-benchmarks); [Innovecs](https://www.innovecsgames.com/blog/mobile-game-retention-benchmarks/)
- **By genre:** mid-core and RPG start lower on D1 but hold better at D7 and D30 — [AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/blog/mobile-game-retention). Board, card, puzzle and casino games do better over the medium and long term, while arcade leads D1 but fades — [GameDevReports on GameAnalytics](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-gaming-benchmarks)

**CPI (vendor and agency data, inconsistent)**
- **Adjust 2025 gaming data:**
  - US about $1.71 per install (+31% vs 2024), APAC about $0.27 — [Sharklio](https://sharklio.com/academy/targeting-bids/cost-per-install-by-country)
  - North America $1.68, Europe $0.53 — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/mobile-app-marketing-statistics-2026-install-data)
- SEA CPI runs at about 0.18x the US — [Linkrunner](https://linkrunner.io/tools/cost-per-install-benchmark)
- US iOS CPI is about $7.68, more than twice APAC's — [adjoe](https://adjoe.io/blog/guide-advertising-mobile-games-across-apac/)
- Mid-core: US iOS about $6.40 vs SEA about $0.90. SEA CPIs are generally 70–85% lower — [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/mobile-game-ua-in-2026-why-cpi-is-rising-and-what-smart-studios-do-differently/)
- Casual and puzzle on Android: US $1.50–$3.50 vs SEA $0.15–$0.60. Indonesia and the Philippines are named among the cheapest markets, and are Android-dominant — [Game Growth Advisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-user-acquisition-cpi-benchmarks-2026/)
- **Conflict:** another benchmark puts US gaming CPI at $5.28 vs Adjust's $1.71 — [Game Growth Advisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-user-acquisition-cpi-benchmarks-2026/)

**Monetization patterns in the genre**
- Clash Royale's 2025 recovery came from reducing reward inflation and rebalancing, not from adding new paywalls — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/ClashRoyale-Comeback)
- MOBA/BR competition is expected to be "play-to-win"; cosmetics are the accepted spend — [Think with Google / Niko](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/10187/Google-Niko-Partners_The-Art-of-Monetizing-Games-in-Asia.pdf)
- Store-tracked revenue per MAU for MLBB is under $2 a year (see section 2). Sensor Tower excludes D2C — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/southeast-asia-mobile-gaming-2025)

### Inferences
- **Test-market economics:** a 10,000-install retention test in Indonesia or the Philippines might cost roughly $1.5K–$9K at $0.15–$0.90 CPI. The same test in the US could cost $17K–$77K. This is illustrative arithmetic from the cited CPI ranges, not a quote.
- **Retention gates:** aim for D1 of at least 35–40% (top decile) and D7 of 10% or more before scaling. Median games at about 4% D7 almost never recover paid UA at SEA ARPU. Expect lower D1 on Android than iOS (25–27% vs 31–33% top-quartile).
- **Payback:** at SEA ARPU of about $19 per player per year (all players, not payers), paid UA in SEA can work even at sub-$1 CPI. Monetization will rely on high-value markets: Malaysia, Thailand, Singapore and Western iOS. A small team should rely on organic, creator and esports-style growth, as Clash Royale did in 2025.

### Gaps
- Searches surfaced no published benchmarks for battle-pass attach rate or price points, the cosmetics vs progression revenue split, or payer conversion for real-time PvP card games.
- No SEA-specific D1/D7/D30 benchmarks were found, and no separate verified CPI for Malaysia, Indonesia or the Philippines.
- No 2025 LTV or ROAS benchmarks were found for strategy/PvP games in SEA.

## 6. Small-team / indie mobile PvP successes, 2022–2026

### Takeaway
Searches found few verified small-team mobile real-time PvP breakouts. The clearest documented example is web-first: Tall Team (8 people, Ireland) built the .io kart-battle game Smash Karts on Poki and CrazyGames, then extended to iOS and Android with cross-progress. TikTok is a proven discovery channel for tiny teams, but the documented cases are not PvP.

### Cited Findings
- **Tall Team / Smash Karts:**
  - Tall Team is a "web-first" independent studio founded in 2017 in Sligo, Ireland, with a team of eight.
  - Smash Karts, an .io-style kart battle game, has over 1M player upvotes on Poki and "2.6 million plays over a 12-month period" per Poki's page (this figure seems low and may refer to a particular window).
  - The game supports accounts with cross-device progress between browser and app.
  - Poki signed the studio's next game, Obby Roads, as a Poki exclusive.
  - Sources: [Poki game page](https://poki.com/en/g/smash-karts); [Poki blog](https://poki.com/blog/tall-teams-launches-obby-roads-poki); [smashkarts.io](https://smashkarts.io/)
  - CrazyGames lists Smash Karts on web (desktop and mobile), iOS and Android — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/smash-karts)
- **Studio Folly (2-person Australian team):** credits TikTok for discovery; one video gained about 10K followers, 70K likes and 300K+ plays. The game is the word game Gubbins, not PvP — [MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/indie-publishing-is-in-a-really-weird-place-on-mobile-so-is-tiktok-the-answer/)
- MobileGamer.biz describes mobile indie publishing as "in a really weird place", with TikTok as a potential answer — [MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/indie-publishing-is-in-a-really-weird-place-on-mobile-so-is-tiktok-the-answer/)
- **Indus (Hashstash, Pune):** an Indian battle royale from a smaller studio. Team size and discovery details were not given — [Tiranga Games App](https://tirangagamesapp.org/indie-indian-game-studios-hidden-gems-viral-hits/)
- **Typical indie team size benchmark:** 5–20 people — [Kevuru Games](https://kevurugames.com/blog/game-development-team-structure-average-team-sizes-and-production-statistics/)
- **Counter-examples:** even large studios failed with new real-time PvP IP. Squad Busters (Supercell) and Warcraft Rumble (Blizzard) were both wound down in 2025 — see section 1.

### Inferences
- The most replicable path for a KL-based small team looks like this:
  1. Build a web/HTML5 or Unity WebGL build first.
  2. Validate the core 1v1 or 2v2 loop on Poki or CrazyGames, which are free distribution with built-in audiences.
  3. Port to mobile with shared accounts.
  4. Soft-launch in Indonesia, the Philippines and Malaysia using cheap installs and creator clips.

  This copies the Tall Team pattern and the Clash Royale organic and creator lesson.
- TikTok and short-form clips favour highly visual, readable "moments" (big card drops, last-second tower saves). The hybrid's design should produce clip-able outcomes every match.

## 7. Platform considerations: Google Play vs App Store in SEA; web, HTML5 and instant-game channels for low-cost testing

### Takeaway
SEA is Android-dominant in volume. MLBB's tracked MAU is about 79% Google Play, and Indonesia is about 87% Android (unverified). Malaysia is the exception, with a large iOS minority (37–46%). Web portals (Poki, CrazyGames) are a proven low-cost test and distribution path for real-time multiplayer. Telegram Mini Apps reach more than 1B users but are dominated by crypto and tap-to-earn titles. Facebook Instant Games remains available but evidence on it is thin.

### Cited Findings
- **MLBB Google Play vs App Store MAU (November 2025):** 45.3M vs 12.1M — [Buffget](https://buffget.com/ja/news/mobile-legends-bang-bang-player-count-november-2025-453m-monthly-stats-and-trends-sg5l0a)
- **Malaysia mobile OS:** Android 63.14% and iOS 36.85% (October 2026, StatCounter). Mobile plus tablet is about 54% / 46% — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/malaysia/monthly-); [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile-tablet/malaysia)
- **Indonesia Android:** about 86.8% (unverified secondary) — [CommandLinux](https://commandlinux.com/android/android-global-market-share-statistics/)
- Sensor Tower SEA download estimates cover the App Store and Google Play only. They exclude pre-installs, re-downloads and third-party Android stores. No store-level revenue split was published in the accessible summary — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/southeast-asia-mobile-gaming-2025)
- Google Play's "Best Game of 2024" in SEA went to Honor of Kings, showing that editorial featuring is regionally curated — [One More Game PH](https://onemoregame.ph/2024/11/honor-of-kings-google-play-best-game-2024/)
- **Telegram:** more than 1B MAU, with a Mini Apps platform for browser games inside chats and bots. Popular titles are mostly tap-to-earn or token-based, such as Hamster Kombat — [Playgama web games market map 2026](https://wiki.playgama.com/playgama/articles/introducing-the-web-games-industry-market-map-2026); [Wikipedia: Hamster Kombat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamster_Kombat)
- **Poki and CrazyGames:**
  - Poki runs a curated catalogue with direct developer partnerships and exclusive deals, such as Tall Team's Obby Roads — [Poki blog](https://poki.com/blog/tall-teams-launches-obby-roads-poki); [Playgama market map](https://wiki.playgama.com/playgama/articles/introducing-the-web-games-industry-market-map-2026)
  - Smash Karts runs on Poki and CrazyGames and on iOS and Android with shared progress — [Poki](https://poki.com/en/g/smash-karts); [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/smash-karts)
- **Facebook Instant Games:** has moved from Messenger into the Facebook app, per a consumer guide; check Meta's documentation for current details — [iTechFever](https://www.itechfever.com/best-facebook-messenger-games/)
- **Messaging-app games history and outlook:** [Speedrun (a16z) substack](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/messaging-app-games)

### Inferences
- **Recommended platform sequence for a KL indie:**
  1. Run a web prototype on Poki or CrazyGames, or a self-hosted .io-style link, to test whether the core loop is fun, measure match completion, and gather early D1 data at near-zero cost.
  2. Ship an Android-first soft launch in the Philippines and Indonesia, the cheapest installs and largest volume.
  3. Launch on iOS plus Android in Malaysia and Thailand to test monetization, since these markets have higher IAP per user and Malaysia has a large iOS share.
  4. Add a D2C web shop or top-up reseller listing (Codashop-style), with e-wallets (Touch 'n Go, GrabPay, Boost, ShopeePay, GCash) and carrier billing.
- Telegram Mini Apps reach a large audience, but its gaming audience skews toward crypto and tap-to-earn. That fits poorly with a skill-based real-time PvP game, though it could be useful for a lightweight async or companion mode.
- Web-portal audiences skew younger and toward desktop and Chromebook play. Web results validate gameplay but will not predict mobile monetization or SEA-specific behaviour.

### Gaps
- Searches surfaced no 2025 Google Play vs App Store revenue split for SEA or Malaysia.
- Poki and CrazyGames monthly-user figures, revenue-share terms and SEA traffic share could not be checked (search budget exhausted).
- No 2025 data on Facebook Instant Games reach in SEA was found, and no case study of a real-time multiplayer game tested via Telegram Mini Apps.
- Roblox was not researched as an alternative channel for small-team PvP prototypes in Indonesia and the Philippines.
