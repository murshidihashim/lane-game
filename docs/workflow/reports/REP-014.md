## SUMMARY

- **Levelling:** the ultimate now comes about a minute and a half in. Before, it came after four
  minutes, and for about half the heroes never.
- **Controls:** five replacements for the pop-up order menu, none of which covers the map. Two use
  cards as orders, not as hand-placed troops, so DEC-011 still holds.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **Levelling (PLAN-011):**
  - Level thresholds lowered to 50, 110 and 180 XP (were 100, 250 and 450).
  - Every living hero now gains 1 XP a second on its own, so a hero on quiet duty still gets its
    ultimate. Fighting, farming and relics still earn about half the XP.
  - New unit tests, a permanent timing check in the soak, and updated help text.
- **Hero controls (DEC-021):**
  1. **Order cards:** tap a hero; a row of order cards replaces the lane chips; drag a card onto
     its target.
  2. **Smart drag:** drag a portrait onto the map; what you drop it on decides the order, and a
     label at your finger shows it first.
  3. **Ring menu:** press and hold a hero; six order icons fan out round your thumb.
  4. **Tactic cards:** a deck of 8 team tactics. Hold 3 and play one with Semangat to order all
     three heroes at once. This is new rules.
  5. **Lane board:** a one-row strip of Left, Mid, Right, Jungle and Home slots; drag hero tokens
     between slots.

  Recommended: 1 with 2 as a shortcut. 4 later, as its own plan, if the owner wants more card
  strategy.
