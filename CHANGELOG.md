# Changelog

_What changed for users of Lane Game. Generated by appkit. Do not edit by hand._

## Unreleased

- First playable prototype: one-on-one against a computer opponent on a three-lane portrait map with a long, winding middle lane.
- Two hero cards (Ranger, Warden) that stay all match, level up and take orders: push, defend, gank, farm, retreat.
- Settings: 2 or 3 lanes, game speed, easy or normal bot.
- 20 heroes from a folk-tale world, each with a Trait, an automatic Power and a level-4 Ultimate.
- Pick 2 heroes before each match; the bot picks 2 at random.
- New effects: stun, silence, pull, push-back, shields, healing, poison, fog, lanterns that reveal, flying, duels, decoys, summons and taking over enemy troops.
- Folk-tale names: Semangat (mana), Pusaka (Core), Silat Guards, Pemanah, Kerbau, Kelip-kelip, Perisai, Petir.
- Pick one of 5 species before the match: Kampung, Hutan, Sungai, Serangga or Batu, each with 3-4 troop classes and its own spell.
- Each lane's waves are your choice: tap a wave chip to change which class marches in that lane.
- New Ward order and map rewards: fruit groves, beast dens (Jungle Fury), the river beast Gajah Mina, relics at the shrines, and bounties for kills and towers.
- New Hikayat art style: a painted rainforest board, timber forts, ink-outlined pieces with symbols, parchment cards and songket trim.
- The jungle species is named Hutan (not Rimba as in the plan) so it does not share a name with the hero Rimba.
- No troop cards: you play by commanding your two heroes and choosing each lane's troops. Both heroes start on the map.
- New Move order: drop a hero anywhere to send it there and hold the spot.
- Shop: buy items for your heroes with Semangat (8 items, 3 slots per hero).
- Ultimates are yours to call: the ULT button glows at a good moment; tap it and the hero fires.
- Fog of war: you only see what your heroes, troops, towers and wards can see.
- Heroes and troops are drawn as characters; every ultimate shows a banner and its own effect; towers show their health.
- Saudagar's ultimate now gives 3 Semangat and halves your next item (it used to make cards cheaper).
- Balance pass: species and heroes evened out (every species now wins 47-58% against the bot, every hero 42-62%), fewer draws and no more very early finishes.
- Fixed: towers now shoot back at long-range attackers that used to hit them from just outside their reach.
- Sudden death: towers take triple damage, so a level match is decided more often.
- Three heroes each, drafted in turns with the bot on a new hero-select screen, then a versus screen before the match.
- Calmer pace: every troop and hero walks about 30% slower; steps now match how far a piece walks.
- Zoom: tap the magnifier, double-tap or pinch the map to look closer (up to 2x), drag to look around.
- Re-balanced for the slower pace: towers 1,700 health, waves grow 18% a minute, sudden death 1:30; species and heroes stay between 42% and 58% against the bot.
- Polished characters: shading, faces, team pennants, smoother walking, breathing, attack swings, hit flashes and arrivals.
- Fixed: heroes no longer stop after an order (they were being pushed back by their own troops or locking up with a teammate).
- Command menu: tap a hero, pick Move, Attack, Push, Gank, Defend, Farm, Ward or Retreat, then tap a highlighted target.
- New Attack order: send a hero after an enemy you can see.
- Smarter heroes: they wait for their troops before walking under an enemy tower, go home to heal when badly hurt, and go for enemy heroes first.
- New look: a batik paper-cut diorama. Paper standees with white cut edges, a batik-printed jungle board, kraft-paper lanes, folded-paper forts, indigo batik fog and menus.
