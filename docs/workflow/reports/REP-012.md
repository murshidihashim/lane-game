## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **Board:** painted once as a paper diorama:
  - jungle cloth printed with batik flowers and wax cracks;
  - cut-paper leaf canopies;
  - kraft-paper lanes with a torn rim;
  - a layered paper river with scalloped edges and batik wave lines;
  - paper bridges, camps, dens and cairns, all with soft paper shadows.
- **Characters:** all 20 heroes and 18 troop types are die-cut paper standees, with a white cut edge,
  a paper shadow and indigo-black ink. Team colour stays on the base and sash.
- **Forts:** folded paper, with batik-dotted roofs in team colours.
- **Fog of war:** indigo dye with faint batik motifs.
- **Menus:** paper cards on indigo batik cloth across the game screen, help, shop, settings, results,
  draft and versus screens.
- **Docs:** `art-style.md` v3, the README and the project rules.
