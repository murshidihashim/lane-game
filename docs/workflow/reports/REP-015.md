## SUMMARY

Hero orders now open as a ring round your thumb (idea 3). Only the ring itself covers part of the
screen, and only while it is open. While you aim, the hint and Cancel take the lane chips' place, so
the map stays clear. The game rules did not change.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **Opening the ring:**
  - Press a hero's panel, and the ring opens just above it.
  - Or hold your hero on the map for a moment, and it opens round your finger. A quicker drag still
    scrolls the map.
  - Eight orders sit on paper discs: Move, Attack, Push, Gank, Defend, Farm, Ward and Retreat. The
    hero's best order is marked ★. The order under your finger rises in gold, and its name shows
    above the ring.
- **Two ways to give an order:**
  - Slide onto an order and let go, then tap a lit-up target. Retreat goes at once.
  - Or do it in one swipe: slide out of the ring through the order and let go on the target. The aim
    point sits just above the finger, so the finger doesn't hide it.
- **Taps:** a quick tap, or letting go without choosing, leaves the ring open for a tap. Tapping its
  middle or outside it closes it.
- **Removed:** the pop-up order menu and the old drag-a-portrait shortcut.
- **Also:** messages move to the top of the map while a ring is open. The help text, owner checklist
  and phone check are updated.
