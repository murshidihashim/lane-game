## SUMMARY

Every hero and troop has been redrawn as a heroic figure in the batik paper style. Dota 2 was the
quality bar, and we followed Valve's published principles: a shape you can read at a glance, and
light at the head going darker towards the feet. Every design is our own, taken from Malay craft,
dress, animals and tales. The game rules are unchanged; the bot-vs-bot results are identical to
before.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **New body template:**
  - about five heads tall, with broad shoulders and longer legs;
  - five builds: slim, stocky, robe, belly and giant;
  - costume parts: pauldrons, chest plates, belts, skirts, bracers, boots and capes.
- **Value pattern:** every figure is lighter at the head and darker at the feet (softer on troops).
- **20 heroes, each with one signature shape**, for example:
  - Kail's giant terendak hat;
  - Benteng's tower shield with our fort emblem;
  - Hujan's oil-paper umbrella;
  - Gergasi's horns and spiked club;
  - Helang's folded wings and great bow;
  - Tukang's bamboo scaffold;
  - Ombak's wave;
  - Dalang's headdress and puppet on glowing strings.

  Magic users and beasts have small glowing eyes or props. Belang's tiger form now wears armour.
- **22 troop and summon types:**
  - militia in helmets with team plumes and tabards;
  - an armoured buffalo ram;
  - beasts and insects with team markings and glowing eyes;
  - a golem with glowing cracks.
- **Size and portraits:** figures are a little bigger (heroes 42 px, troops 24 px), and portraits
  are framed for the smaller heads.
- **Docs:**
  - `docs/design/character-art.md` lists each hero's source and signature shape;
  - the art-style guide (v4) has a new character section.
