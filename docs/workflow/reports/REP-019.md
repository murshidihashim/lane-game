## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

**Action**
- **Aim your ultimate:** hold an ULT ⤴ button and drag it onto the map. 11 ultimates aim at:
  - an enemy (Hunter's Mark, Grand Performance, Duel, Coil);
  - one of your towers (Unbreakable);
  - a spot (Riptide, Moon Kite, Stampede, Dawn Freeze);
  - a lane (Flash Flood, King Tide).

  A tap still fires the ultimate at the next good moment.
- **Warning rings:**
  - Riptide, Moon Kite, Stampede and Dawn Freeze land 1 s after a ring appears.
  - Duel and Coil lock on after 0.6 s and miss a hero that gets away.
  - Gajah Mina now slams the ground every 7 s while fought, with a ring first.
  - Both sides see every ring, red for the enemy's and gold for yours. A ring fizzles if its caster falls.
- **Dodge dash:** flick one of your heroes on the map to dash 400 that way, once every 10 s.
  A "» dash" mark on the hero's panel shows when it is ready.
- **Power on cue:** each panel has a ✦ gem. When the Power is ready it waits 1.5 s for you: a tap
  fires it 20% stronger. Untouched, it fires by itself as before.
- **Combo chains:** Power and ultimate damage from different heroes on the same enemy within 2 s
  adds +15%, then +30%, with a red-ink COMBO stamp.

**Strategy**
- **Rally the waves:** each lane chip has a HOLD toggle.
  - Held waves wait at your end of the lane, up to 3.
  - GO sends them all together: 25% faster and 15% harder for 8 s.
  - The 4th wave releases them by itself.
  - The enemy sees the build-up only with vision there.
- **Level-up choices:** at levels 2 and 3 the level badge glows. Tap it to choose:
  - the hero's own upgrade;
  - or a general one: Quick (the Power comes back 25% sooner) at level 2, Strong (+30%) at level 3.

  After 8 s the hero's own upgrade is picked for you.
- **Commander calls (DEC-024):** pick 2 of 6 on the "Choose your people" screen:
  - War Drum, Bamboo Barricade, Spirit Eye, Penawar, Snare Trap and Fort Repair.
  - They cost Semangat, so they compete with items, and then cool down.
  - Two buttons sit beside the Shop.

**Also**
- Help text, owner checklist, roster docs, art-style guide and CLAUDE.md are updated.
- A bug from PLAN-013 is fixed: tapping a hero's edge tab could open the order ring of another hero standing under it.

## NOT DONE OR NOT VERIFIED

- Not tried on a real phone. With three heroes, the new buttons could be too much to watch, which
  is the main risk the research named.
- The bot uses the same moves but less cleverly than a person. In particular, it never aims its
  ultimates; it uses the tap mode.
- Some numbers changed with the rings: auto-fired Riptide now hits within 700 of Kail, and the dash
  is 400 (was planned as 320, so a dash can leave a ring).
