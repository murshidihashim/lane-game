## SUMMARY

Before a match you now pick a **species** as well as 2 heroes. The species decides:
- your troop cards
- your spell
- what marches in each lane

Each lane's wave is your choice. Heroes take a new **Ward** order, and the map now pays heroes to
explore it. The whole game has one look, "Hikayat": a painted folk-tale board game.

## PLAN OR WORK COMPLETED

- **Species (DEC-008):**
  - **Kampung**: Silat Guard, Pemanah, Perisai, Kerbau; spell Gotong-royong.
  - **Hutan**: Kera, Harimau Bintang, Enggang, Seladang; spell Auman.
  - **Sungai**: Ketam, Ikan Sumpit, Belut; spell Arus.
  - **Serangga**: Kerengga, Tebuan, Kumbang, Ratu Sarang; spell Sengat.
  - **Batu**: Kerikil, Batu Besar, Lontar; spell Gempa.
  - Each class has an ability, such as flying, splash, armour, slow, poison, chain, charge, leap,
    burst or hatching.
- **Lane waves:**
  - Every 20 s each lane sends a wave of the class you picked for it, at 60% strength.
  - Tap a lane's wave chip to change its class.
  - Your deck is your species' classes, its spell, Petir and your 2 heroes.
- **Orders and map rewards (DEC-009):** Push, Gank, Defend, Farm, Ward, Retreat, and Collect relic.
  The map pays through:
  - 2 fruit groves (Semangat)
  - 2 beast dens (Jungle Fury)
  - the river beast Gajah Mina at 1:30, which joins whoever beats it
  - relics every 45 s
  - 6 ward cairns
  - bounties for hero kills, the first kill and towers
- **Art (DEC-010):**
  - A painted rainforest map, timber kubu forts and the Pusaka pavilion.
  - Ink-outlined tokens with a symbol for each class.
  - Parchment cards and menus with songket trim.
  - Guide: `docs/design/art-style.md`.
- **Bot:** picks a species, changes its lane waves, wards, farms dens, hunts Gajah Mina and
  collects relics.
- **Docs:** `docs/design/species.md` is generated from the game data.
